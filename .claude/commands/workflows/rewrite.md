Rewrite the text I name below so it serves its readers. If I haven't named a target, ask me for one.

Start from what the text is for and who reads it, then from its source material. Follow the repository's writing guidance in AGENTS.md, and use skill `clear-writing`, including its part on writing for AI models when the reader is a model. You may change what the text asks its reader to do when that serves its purpose better, but keep the facts and the rules that protect something.

When you have a draft, have one subagent with a fresh context read it as its intended reader and compare it with the original. It reports what doesn't work for that reader and every fact or rule that changed or disappeared. Then revise the draft.

Leave your changes uncommitted, and tell me briefly what changed, and point out where the text now says or asks for something different. Don't post anything on GitHub.
