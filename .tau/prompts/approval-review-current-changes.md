Use @@skill:approval-review. Review the current changes for a decision-maker who has not read their supporting discussion, diff, or source. Investigate the complete target first, then guide me through the material decisions from broadest to narrowest, one fully resolved question at a time. Do not ask the first question until the investigation and decision map are complete.
